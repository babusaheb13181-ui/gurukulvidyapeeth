📚 Introduction

GURUKUL VIDYAPEETH is a unique English medium Co-educational Public School, affiliated to C.B.S.E Delhi. It runs under the direct control of Gurukul Educational and Welfare Trust (GEWT) and under guidance of a team of the most reputed Principals of the prestigious public schools of India. The courses of study are the same as prescribed by the Central Board of Secondary Education, Delhi.

💫 Our Neo Humanistic Outlook & Ambition Towards Value Education

The Philosophy of Neo-Humanistic education is based on extending the inherent love of human beings to all people, animals, plants and the inanimate world. This outlook is ideally suited to the needs and changing vision of today's society with its shifts from a mechanistic world view to one based on holistic and collective harmony.

We aim to provide programme in which children will develop a universal outlook, free from all discrimination. They come to embrace all people as one human family, regardless of race, religion, colour, creed or gender. Through such activities as gardening, nature studies and care for animals, a true ecological consciousness guides them to protect and nurture their environment and realize their inter-relationship with nature. Cultivation of the arts and music is encouraged and children are provided with a congenial environment in order to awaken their creative potential.

Neo-Humanistic education promotes unity in diversity, the use of science and technology for the welfare of all and maintaining an ecologically balanced world. By cultivating this universal outlook, Neo-Humanistic education strives to check the growth of narrow sentiments and exploitation which currently divide the world. We believe in rationality and not superstition, dogma or fanaticism. We aim to tap the unique capacity of human beings to think rationally, creatively and intuitively.

📝 Admission

(a) Admission will be open for those classes in which the vacancies will be cropping up.

(b) The candidates are selected for admission after qualifying a written test/interview or both.

(c) The academic session will commence from April every year.

(d) A child must have completed 06 years of age for admission to Class-I as on 31 March. Age for other classes can be calculated accordingly. The photocopy for proof of age (Birth Certificate issued by Municipal Corporation, Panchayat, Hospital/Nursing Home duly attested by a competent authority and school leaving certificate). Two recent passport size colour photographs each of the candidate and his/her parent/legal guardian.

(e) Any chronic and communicable disease is held as bar to admission.

(g) Students belonging to indecent as well as non-serious parent will not be admitted or may be discontinued even after admission also.

Students after joining the school, if found undesirable due to any harmful habits are liable to be discontinued.

25% seats are reserved in class 1 for the children belonging to BPL category or for the instructions of the Govt. of Bihar/India.

(k) The School is keeping up with the RTE Act 2009. Admissions in this connection are taken by relevant process. The aspirants may contact school office in march every year.
